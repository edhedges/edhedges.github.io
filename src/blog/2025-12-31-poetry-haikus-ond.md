Here we are at the final installment of my haiku series, and what better time to wrap it up than the last day of the year? This time, I'll be focusing on the months of October, November, and December. Part 3 can be read [here](/blog/2025/12/30/poetry-haikus-jas).

---

### October
Fest season arrives

The leaves falling through crisp air

Ending with a scare

### November
Starting to reflect

Thankful for many bounties

The cold draws us close

### December
Hang lights, deck the halls

Sing the tunes, breath the tree

Christmastime is here

---

And so, with minutes to spare, I close out this year-long journey through the months. There's something fitting about finishing on December 31st—nothing like waiting until the last second to get things done! These final months bring the cozy descent into winter: the crispness of October, the gratitude of November, and the magic of December. It's been a rewarding exercise to pause and reflect on what each month means to me, one haiku at a time.
