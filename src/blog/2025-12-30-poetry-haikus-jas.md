I'm back with the third installment of my haiku series! As the year winds down, I'm catching up on these quarterly reflections. This time, I'll be focusing on the months of July, August, and September. Part 2 can be read [here](/blog/2025/12/02/poetry-haikus-amj).

---

### July
My first child was born

Just by chance my birth as well

Favorite of mine

### August
Peak heat in session

The water is our savior

Is summer over

### September
My wife's birthday month

Summer ends, crisp autumn drops

Holidays to come

---

These months hold a special place in my heart. July marks the birthdays of both my first child and myself, making it an easy pick for my favorite month. August brings the peak of summer heat, driving us to pools, lakes, and anywhere with water. And September ushers in my wife's birthday along with that first hint of autumn air, signaling the holiday season ahead.
